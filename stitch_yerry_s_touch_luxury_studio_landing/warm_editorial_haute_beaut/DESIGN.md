## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-glamour, bespoke bridal and high-fashion beauty aesthetic tailored for luxury clientele in Addis Ababa and internationally. Rooted in warm romanticism, artisanal precision, and bridal couture elegance, the visual atmosphere bridges modern editorial poise with tactile architectural grace inspired by salon fluting, delicate brass fixtures, and soft bridal draping.

### Design Persona & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** High-profile bridal parties, contemporary Ethiopian diasporic brides, editorial models, and private clients seeking transcendent, red-carpet glam and master-level skin artistry.
- **Tone & Mood:** Warm, poised, opulent, welcoming, and meticulous. It whispers timeless luxury rather than loud ostentation.
- **Visual Expression:** Modern Editorial Glassmorphism combined with Tactile Architectural details (subtle vertical ribbing, delicate gold leaf hairline frames, and light-reflecting champagne specular touches).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the warm ivory tones of couture gowns, radiant skin preps, polished champagne metallic lettering, and grounded espresso shadows.

- **Primary Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Represents prestige, radiance, and master-level certified craft. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and delicate borders.
- **Secondary Deep Espresso (`#1E1513`):** Provides editorial contrast far richer and warmer than clinical pitch black. Anchors headlines, primary text, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Tertiary Soft Warm Gold (`#E5C378`):** Highlights, gradient shimmers, button hover states, and ambient radial glow accents.
- **Neutral Ivory / Alabaster (`#FAF7F2`):** The primary canvas background, replacing stark pure white with gentle, bridal silk warmth.
- **Accent Rose Cream (`#F7EBE8`):** Subtle card fills, nested pill backgrounds, and badge surfaces to introduce soft flushed warmth.

## Typography

Typography balances the romantic, statuesque prestige of high-fashion editorial print with contemporary digital clarity.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**, evoking classic Vogue and Harper's Bazaar editorial spreads. Titles feature slightly tightened tracking and intentional italic accents for specialized terminology (e.g., *Bridal Glamour*, *L’Atelier*).
- **Body & Captions:** Handled by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric balance, generous apertures, and high legibility against tinted ivory backgrounds.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Uppercase styling in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with wide tracking (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to echo high-end parfum packaging and boutique signage.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the open, unhurried cadence of an elite private salon suite.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max-content width), shifting to a 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column column layout on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` on hero and transition zones) establishes an uncrowded luxury pacing.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Lookbook layouts and portfolio showcases utilize subtle vertical offsets (alternate cards dropped by 32px) to break rigid corporate boxes and evoke fashion lookbooks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines light-refracting glassmorphic planes with warm, diffuse radiance rather than harsh grayscale drop shadows.

- **Background Atmosphere:** Layered subtle radial gradients of warm gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`) and rose glow behind ivory planes.
- **Fluted Texture Layers:** Subtle linear SVG backdrops mimicking white vertical salon counter fluting (`rgba(30, 21, 19, 0.02)` stripes spaced at 12px intervals).
- **Glassmorphic Glass Cards:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` background fills with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp hairline perimeter: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`.
- **Specular Glow:** Active cards and floating floating elements cast a soft champagne halo: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 21, 19, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined balance of soft architectural curvature and structured editorial framing.

- **Cards and Containers:** Feature a `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) radius, softening tactile touchpoints while retaining structural poise.
- **Boutique Badges & Buttons:** Expressed either as sculpted `rounded-full` (curved pill forms for beauty tags and floating appointment badges) or strictly clipped `0.5rem` shapes for booking date pickers.
- **Image Frames:** Utilize subtle Romanesque arched top borders (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px`) on select portrait transformations and artist feature windows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Couture Gold):** Filled with solid `#D4AF37` or champagne gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E5C378 0%, #D4AF37 100%)`), text set in `#1E1513` in bold uppercase `label-lg`, wrapped with `0.5rem` rounded corners and subtle shadow.
- **Secondary (Editorial Ghost):** Frosted ivory surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), 1px border in gold foil tone (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`), deep espresso typography, shifting to full gold on hover.

### Service & Lookbook Cards
- Constructed with frosted glass panels, an inner 1px champagne border, and padding of `space-lg`.
- Image containers feature a warm vignette and 4:5 editorial aspect ratios. 
- Service pricing badges use pill chips in soft rose cream (`#F7EBE8`) with micro gold foil borders.

### Fluted Section Dividers & Accent Trims
- Architectural divider lines with a central golden rhombus ornament or 1px hairline gold gradient tapering out towards transparent margins.

### Inputs & Date Selection
- Input fields are finished in translucent alabaster (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), deep espresso text, with floating labels in `label-md`. Focused states transition to a 1.5px gold glow outline.

### Testimonial & Masterclass Badges
- Floating quote capsules highlighting certified masterclass credentials, bridal testimonials, and studio location in Addis Ababa with golden starburst glyphs.
