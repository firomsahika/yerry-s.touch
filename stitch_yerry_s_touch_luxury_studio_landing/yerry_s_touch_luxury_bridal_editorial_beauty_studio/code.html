✦ Artisan Biography

Where Ethiopian Heritage Meets Haute Couture Skin

Led by certified makeup artist Yeron Habtamu, Yerry's Touch was founded on a simple yet elevated philosophy: high-glamour beauty should honor your natural facial architecture, not mask it.

From the regal significance of traditional Ethiopian wedding ceremonies (Melse and royal crowning celebrations) to red-carpet editorial events at Addis Ababa’s premier venues, Yeron combines deep color theory with clinical skincare prep to create looks that stay velvety, luminous, and camera-ready throughout 16+ hours of joyous celebration.

"Every face holds a unique, divine glow. My passion is simply bringing it into the light so that when a bride glances in the mirror, she sees her most transcendent self."

— Yeron Habtamu, Founder & Creative Director
